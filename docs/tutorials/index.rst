=========
Tutorials
=========

Lessons that take you by the hand through getting django-finder running and doing something
useful with it. They are learning-oriented: follow them in order, type the commands as they
are written, and do not worry yet about why every step works — the :doc:`../explanation/index`
section covers that.

If you already have django-finder running and want to accomplish one specific thing, you want
the :doc:`../how-to/index` instead.
